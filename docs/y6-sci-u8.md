# Year 6 Science — Unit 8: Food Preservation Technology

## 1. Food Spoilage

**Food spoilage** means food has become bad and unsafe to eat.

Spoilt food can be detected using our senses:

| Sense | What we notice                              |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Sight | Food becomes mouldy, changes colour         |
| Smell | Food smells bad or rotten                   |
| Taste | Food has an unpleasant taste                |
| Touch | Food changes texture, becomes slimy or soft |

**Important:** Spoilt food is **unsafe for consumption**.

---

## 2. Characteristics of Spoilt Food

Spoilt food may show these signs:

1. **Smells bad**
2. **Tastes unpleasant**
3. **Changes colour**
4. **Changes texture**
5. **Becomes mouldy**

Example sentence:

**The bread is spoilt because it has mould and smells bad.**

---

## 3. Why Food Becomes Spoilt

Food spoilage happens because of the **action of microorganisms**.

Microorganisms such as **bacteria** and **fungi** grow easily on food that is:

* moist
* exposed to air
* not stored properly

Example:

**Bacteria and fungi grow on moist bread when it is exposed to air.**

---

# 4. Food Preservation

## Meaning of Food Preservation

**Food preservation** is the process of slowing down food from getting spoilt.

Food preservation helps to:

* inhibit the activity of microorganisms
* slow down the growth of microorganisms
* make food last longer

---

# 5. Methods of Food Preservation

## A. Boiling

**Boiling** uses high temperature to kill microorganisms such as bacteria and fungi.

Examples:

* rendang
* jam

Exam sentence:

**Boiling preserves food because high temperature kills microorganisms.**

---

## B. Drying

**Drying** removes water from food.

Microorganisms cannot survive or become inactive when there is no water.

Food can be dried:

* under the sun
* in an oven

Examples:

* fish
* prawns
* squid
* fruits

Exam sentence:

**Drying preserves food by removing water, so microorganisms cannot grow easily.**

---

## C. Pickling

**Pickling** means soaking food in:

* vinegar
* sugar solution
* salt solution

High acidity or high concentration of sugar or salt prevents microorganisms from growing.

Examples:

* fruits
* vegetables

Exam sentence:

**Pickling preserves food because vinegar, sugar or salt prevents the growth of microorganisms.**

---

## D. Vacuum Packing

**Vacuum packing** removes air from food packaging.

Microorganisms cannot reproduce without air inside the packaging.

Examples:

* meat
* sausages
* mushrooms

Exam sentence:

**Vacuum packing preserves food by removing air so microorganisms cannot reproduce.**

---

## E. Freezing and Cooling

### Freezing

Food is stored at **0°C and below**.

Examples:

* chicken
* meat
* prawns
* squid

### Cooling

Food is stored at a low temperature, around **4°C**.

Examples:

* fruits
* vegetables
* milk

Low temperature makes microorganisms less active and slows down their growth.

Exam sentence:

**Freezing and cooling preserve food because low temperature slows down the growth of microorganisms.**

---

## F. Salting

**Salting** means covering food with a large quantity of salt.

Salt removes moisture from food and prevents microorganisms from growing.

Examples:

* fish
* eggs
* vegetables

Exam sentence:

**Salting preserves food by removing moisture from food.**

---

## G. Waxing

**Waxing** means coating fruits and vegetables with liquid wax.

This prevents microorganisms from growing on the surface and keeps the food fresh.

Examples:

* apples
* oranges
* tomatoes

Exam sentence:

**Waxing preserves food by coating the surface and preventing microorganisms from growing.**

---

## H. Smoking

**Smoking** dries food using smoke.

This method takes a long time.

Examples:

* fish
* meat
* bananas

Exam sentence:

**Smoking preserves food by drying the food and reducing moisture.**

---

## I. Pasteurising

**Pasteurising** uses heat to kill microorganisms without changing the taste or nutritional value of food.

The food is heated at:

* **63°C for 30 minutes**, or
* **72°C for 15 seconds**

Then it is cooled immediately at **4°C**.

Examples:

* milk
* fruit juice

Exam sentence:

**Pasteurising preserves food by heating it to kill microorganisms and then cooling it quickly.**

---

## J. Canning and Bottling

**Canning and bottling** involve cooking food at high temperature to kill microorganisms and storing it in airtight cans or bottles.

Examples:

* meat
* fruits
* vegetables

Exam sentence:

**Canning and bottling preserve food by killing microorganisms and sealing the food in airtight containers.**

---

# 6. Importance of Food Preservation Technology

Food preservation is important because it:

1. **Makes food last longer**
   Food has a longer shelf life.

2. **Allows food to be eaten all year round**
   Seasonal fruits can be enjoyed throughout the year.

3. **Makes food easier to store and transport**
   Food can be sent to other places more easily.

4. **Prevents food wastage**
   Less food is thrown away.

5. **Ensures food supply during disasters**
   Food can be sent to places affected by floods or earthquakes.

# 8. Common Exam Answer Patterns

## Question: Why does food become spoilt?

**Answer:**
Food becomes spoilt because microorganisms such as bacteria and fungi grow on the food.

---

## Question: Why does drying preserve food?

**Answer:**
Drying removes water from food. Microorganisms cannot grow easily without water.

---

## Question: Why does freezing preserve food?

**Answer:**
Freezing uses very low temperature to slow down the growth of microorganisms.

---

## Question: Why does vacuum packing preserve food?

**Answer:**
Vacuum packing removes air from the packaging. Microorganisms cannot reproduce easily without air.

---

## Question: State one importance of food preservation.

**Answer:**
Food preservation makes food last longer and prevents food wastage.

---

# 9. Key Points to Remember

* Spoilt food is unsafe to eat.
* Spoilt food may smell bad, taste bad, change colour, change texture or become mouldy.
* Food spoilage is caused by microorganisms.
* Microorganisms grow easily on moist food exposed to air.
* Food preservation slows down food spoilage.
* Preservation methods work by controlling **water, air, temperature, salt, sugar, acidity or sealing**.
* The main purpose is to stop or slow down the growth of microorganisms.
